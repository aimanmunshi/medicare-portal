❤ Heart disease awareness

Heart disease is the leading cause of death worldwide, but most cases can be prevented with early detection and healthy habits. Knowing the warning signs can save a life.

Common conditions

Coronary artery disease Heart attack Heart failure Arrhythmia High blood pressure

Warning signs

Chest pain or pressure Shortness of breath Pain in arm or jaw Unusual fatigue Dizziness

Prevention tips

✓ Exercise 30 minutes most days
✓ Eat less salt, sugar and fried food
✓ Do not smoke
✓ Check blood pressure regularly

Educational information only, not medical advice. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.
